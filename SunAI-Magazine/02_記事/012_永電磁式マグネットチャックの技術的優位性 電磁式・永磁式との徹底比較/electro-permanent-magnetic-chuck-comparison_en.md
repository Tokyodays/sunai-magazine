## 12. Why Permanent Magnetic Chucks Outperform Electromagnetic and Electro-Permanent Types: A Data-Driven Comparison

### 1. Understanding the Structural Difference Between the Three Types

The performance gap between chuck types is not a matter of product quality — it is a direct consequence of how each type generates its magnetic force. Understanding the operating principle of each explains why their performance diverges in precision machining conditions.

#### 1-1. Electromagnetic Chuck — Magnetism Exists Only While Current Flows

An electromagnetic chuck generates its holding field by passing continuous current through wound coils. No current, no field — instantly. This makes the design simple and scalable to large formats, which is why electromagnetic chucks are common on surface grinders.

But "continuous current" creates three unavoidable problems in precision machining. First, coil resistance generates continuous heat, causing the chuck body to thermally expand during operation. Second, any power interruption — planned or unplanned — releases the workpiece immediately. Third, permanent wiring is required at all times, making electromagnetic chucks incompatible with rotary applications.

#### 1-2. Electro-Permanent Chuck — Better Than Electromagnetic, But Still Electrically Dependent

Electro-permanent chucks combine neodymium and AlNiCo magnets, using a brief electrical pulse to switch the AlNiCo's polarity and toggle the surface field ON or OFF. Power consumption during the hold cycle is zero — an improvement over electromagnetic. But the switching cable remains a permanent connection: repeated plug/unplug cycles lead to cable fatigue and failure, a well-documented weakness of this design. The dual-magnet construction also adds cost and weight compared to pure permanent magnetic designs.

#### 1-3. Permanent Magnetic Chuck — The Magnet Is the Source

A permanent magnetic chuck uses neodymium magnets as the sole source of magnetic force. In the ON state, magnets align in alternating N-S stripes to generate a surface field. In the OFF state, rotating the switching shaft brings adjacent magnets into internal cancellation — the surface field collapses. No current. No coil. No cable.

Every Sun Ai magnetic chuck operates on this principle. The company manufactures no electromagnetic chucks and no electro-permanent chucks. This is not a product gap — it is a deliberate engineering commitment to the design that best serves precision machining requirements.

Sun Ai's permanent magnetic chucks are built with upper yoke laminates cut by shearing (not stamping) and bonded individually with adhesive, sealing the laminate stack against coolant ingress over years of service. A gear-based switching mechanism allows intermediate force positions — half rotation, half force — and distributes mechanical load across 1–6 switching shaft locations depending on chuck size, preventing single-point wear.

## まとめ

The three structural advantages of Sun Ai's permanent magnetic chucks over electromagnetic and electro-permanent alternatives:
